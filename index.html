របៀបលេង · How to play

  • ចុចតាមចង្វាក់ស្គរ
    Press your key exactly when a drum note reaches the golden ring. Your crew paddles in time with you.
  • ភាពស៊ីចង្វាក់ (Sync)
    Perfect and Good hits raise crew sync → more speed. Misses or stray taps shake the crew and slow the boat.
  • កម្លាំងនាគ (Naga Power)
    Every 10 hits in a row unleashes a golden speed burst!
  • ដល់ទីបញ្ចប់មុនគេ
    Be first across the finish line in front of the Royal Palace.
